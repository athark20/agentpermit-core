# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

In short: we are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and professional experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. Be respectful, assume good intent, and disagree constructively.

## Expected behavior
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Report violations to **athar.khan@agentpermit.net**. Maintainers will
review and respond, and may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a permanent ban.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1 is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
